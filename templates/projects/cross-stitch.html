The assignment

A course project on the full website design and build process, with one constraint: use existing libraries, so that programming is practised as a collaborative activity rather than a solo one.

I built a tool for designing cross stitch patterns — or any block-based pattern. You can drop a photograph behind the grid to trace from, and pick from the official DMC thread colours rather than approximating them on screen.

I worked through three versions in Figma first. Exploring layout and colour there let me discard two directions quickly instead of discovering their problems in code. The design shown here is the one the build is based on.
